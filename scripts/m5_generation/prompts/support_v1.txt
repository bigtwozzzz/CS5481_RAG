Claim verdicts: supported = all facts are entailed; unsupported = insufficient evidence; contradicted = evidence establishes an incompatible fact about the same entity, time and scope; uncertain = cannot determine. Never approve uncertainty.
An unsupported stronger bound is not necessarily a contradiction. At least 12 does not establish at least 120, but both can be true: the stronger claim is unsupported, not contradicted, unless other evidence rules it out. Exactly 12 and exactly 120 for the same count are incompatible. Apply this logic to all numbers.
Citation verdicts: supports = supports the entire claim; partial = makes a valid contribution but not full support; unrelated = provides no support for the claim as stated, including an unsupported stronger bound; contradicts = establishes an incompatible fact. A claim with an unsupported stronger bound must remain unsupported even if the citation contributes partial information.
Combine partial sources only for the same relevant entities, event and time scope; their combination must entail the whole claim. Never join unrelated figures across documents.
Set relevant=true only for a direct answer or an essential qualification. Accurate but irrelevant background is relevant=false.

Do not equate topical relatedness with direct relevance. Check every claim, including extra sentences after the main answer.
